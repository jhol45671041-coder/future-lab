    Safety rules & the parent deal

    The rules are not the boring part — they're what makes a parent say yes. Read them together before the first job.

    For parents: the 10-minute setup

      Kid + Parent agreement

      This agreement is between ________ (the owner), their parent, and ________ (the business). We all agree to these rules:

      • Jobs are only accepted with a parent's knowledge, and the customer is someone our family knows or has met.
      • Hours worked never get in the way of school, sleep, or family plans.
      • All earnings get split 50 / 35 / 15 — that's save / spend / give — unless we both agree to change it.
      • Either of us can pause the business at any time to fix a problem.
      • Every safety rule above gets followed on every single job, even when it feels unnecessary.
